# guide-paid-ads-overview — Paid ads, on autopilot

Hook: the paid side of Ryze — Google and Meta in one dashboard, an agent
that audits nonstop, finds the leaks in dollars, and applies fixes with
your one click.

## Laws
- Content changes only from a visible click. No teleports.
- Every VO fact is on screen when spoken.
- The agent panel opens by navbar click, then persists.

## Click script

BEAT 1 — Dashboard (VO 01)
  Screen: Paid Ads Dashboard, Overview tab: Spend / Results / CPA / Clicks
  KPIs, Spend&ROAS + Conversions + CTR combo charts, Channels table with
  Google and Meta rows. No clicks.

BEAT 2 — Connect (VO 02)
  1. Click rail "Integrations" -> integrations page.
  2. Click filter tab "Advertising" -> only ad platforms remain: Google
     Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, Microsoft.

BEAT 3 — Ask the agent (VO 03)
  3. Click rail "Dashboard" (Paid Ads group) -> dashboard again.
  4. Click navbar "Agent" -> panel slides in with suggestions.
  5. Click panel composer. Type "Where am I overspending?"
  6. Click Send. Tools: Google Ads + Meta insights run; answer + metrics
     card: three leaks with $/mo each.

BEAT 4 — Approvals (VO 04)
  7. Click rail "Approvals" -> board of findings (panel stays, board
     clips at the main edge).

BEAT 5 — Creatives (VO 05)
  8. Click rail "Ad Creatives" -> library masonry under the creatives
     line.

BEAT 6 — Inspiration tour + close (VO 05b, 06)
  9. Click rail "Ad Templates" on "grab" -> templates masonry.
  10. Click rail "Competitor Ads" on "peek" -> explore wall.
  Close line over competitors, then outro lockup. Panel persists
  through all three pages.
