# guide-competitor-ads

Hook: your competitors already told you which ads work — they keep paying for them.
Setup: Competitor Ads page, Explore tab — a live wall of ads from your industry; filter it down (platform, format).
Work: Tracked tab → Discover competitors dialog → pick 3 brands → chips + their live ads; open one ad in the lightbox — "Running 91 days" means it converts, nobody pays three months for a loser.
Wow: Generate similar → the agent drafts YOUR version of a proven ad in your brand style.
Close: turn on new-ad alerts (bell) — you'll know the day a competitor launches something new. Watching, copying, improving — on autopilot.

Flow of clicks (every fact = an action):
1. nav Competitor Ads → Explore wall
2. filter Platform → Meta, Format → Image (wall refilters)
3. tab Tracked Competitors → empty → Discover competitors dialog
4. pick 3 brands → Track selected → chips appear, wall fills (one chip "Syncing…")
5. chip yankeecandle → brand-filtered wall
6. click an ad card → lightbox (Format / Running 91 days / Platform / Status Active)
7. Generate similar → agent panel drafts variations
8. close panel → bell on chip → alerts
9. close
