# guide-writing-articles — How articles are written, and how to make them yours

Hook: articles write themselves — but you decide how they read and look.
Setup: open an article from the Content Plan — title, body, images, score, all generated from a real keyword.
Work 1 (per-article): edit the article — swap the featured image, insert products from your store, set tags, add a custom FAQ.
Work 2 (settings): SEO Settings — article language, article length, tone of voice, custom writing instructions (type a rule), article examples.
Work 3 (visuals): image style — Photo Realistic / Illustration / Sketch / Watercolor, then Your style: upload reference images — every future article's images come out in YOUR style.
Close: from here every article the machine writes is unmistakably yours.
