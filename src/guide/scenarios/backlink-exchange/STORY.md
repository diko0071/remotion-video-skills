# guide-backlink-exchange

Hook: Google ranks sites other sites link to. You can't buy those links — but you can trade for them.
Setup: an article preview with a LIVE exchange link in the body (anchor "hand-poured soy candles" linking out to a partner) — this is what an exchange link looks like in the wild: contextual, inside real content.
Teach: chains, never swaps — a linen site links to you, you link to a coffee site; everyone's domain rating grows, Google sees nothing to punish. Non-competing clients only.
Where: Mentions → Backlinks tab — every link earned, referring URL → your target page, per month.
Default: SEO Settings → Publishing → "Backlink exchange" toggle is ON out of the box — you're in the network from day one (and can leave right here).
Close: up to 50 backlinks a month on autopilot; the whole network wins.

Clicks: article preview (link highlighted) → nav Mentions → tab Backlinks → nav Settings (toggle in frame) → close.
