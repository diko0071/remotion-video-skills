# guide-platform-overview — Ryze in ninety seconds

Hook: connect once, ask the agent, and your marketing runs — paid and
organic in one place. Top-level storytelling only: NO module details.

## Laws
- Content changes only from a visible click. No teleports.
- Every VO fact is on screen when spoken.
- Drill-in changes only the rail.
- Machine-gun tours: one click per named thing, ~1.5-2s per page.

## Click script

BEAT 1 — Connect (VO 01)
  Screen: workspace Home.
  1. Click rail "Integrations" on "connect" -> integrations page (analytics,
     site, ad platforms cards).

BEAT 2 — The agent builds everything (VO 02)
  2. Click navbar "Agent" -> panel slides in with suggestions over
     Integrations.

BEAT 3 — Paid tour (VO 03)
  3. Click rail "Dashboard" -> paid dashboard (Google+Meta KPIs).
  4. Click rail "Ad Creatives" -> creatives library.
  5. Click rail "Ad Templates" -> templates wall.
  6. Click rail "Competitor Ads" -> competitor wall.

BEAT 4 — Organic tour (VO 04)
  7. Click rail "SEO" (drill-in, rail only).
  8. Click rail item "Dashboard" -> SEO dashboard.
  9. Click tab "GEO" -> GEO dashboard.
  10. Click rail item "Content Plan" -> articles table.
  11. Click rail item "Technical Audit" -> audit page.

BEAT 5 — Close (VO 05)
  12. Click rail back arrow (rail returns to main).
  13. Click rail "Home" -> Home under the close line. Outro.
