# guide-agent — One agent. Your whole marketing.

Hook: meet the agent — ask anything in plain words, it answers with your
real numbers. Then the point: it is not a page, it lives in the sidebar on
every screen, and everything you can see, it can change.

## Laws this script obeys
- A rail click on SEO (drill-in) changes ONLY the menu. Content changes
  ONLY when a menu item or a row is clicked.
- Every VO fact is visible on screen the moment it is spoken. The article
  edit happens on VISIBLE intro text (scrolled into view before the ask).
- The agent panel, once opened, stays open across every page (product
  behaviour). Its thread accumulates.
- Every screen change is caused by a visible click. No teleports.

## Click script (number = cursor click)

BEAT 1 — Chat (VO 01, 02)
  Screen: empty chat: "Rank higher on Google" hero, composer, playbooks.
  1. Click composer -> caret. Type "Where did our organic revenue come
     from last month?"
  2. Click Send. Turn appears; tools run: Search Console -> Analytics ->
     Shopify; answer text; bar chart (left-aligned labels).
  No clicks while the answer plays.

BEAT 2 — Brand, live edit (VO 03, 04)
  3. Click rail "Brand" -> content: Brand Identity (Offer / Industry /
     Tone of voice cards). Rail stays main.
  4. Click navbar "Agent" -> panel slides in with empty-state suggestions.
  5. Click panel composer. Type "Rewrite our tone of voice — warmer, more
     handmade".
  6. Click Send. Tool row "Update brand"; answer; the Tone of voice card
     text SWAPS in place. Hold 2s on the swapped card.

BEAT 3 — Article, edit on visible text (VO 05, 06)
  7. Click rail "SEO" (drill-in). ONLY the rail changes to the SEO menu.
     Brand content stays put.
  8. Click rail item "Content Plan" -> content: articles table.
  9. Click row "Why your candle tunnels and how to fix it" -> editor.
     Panel stays open. Scroll down so the H1 + the intro paragraph are
     both fully visible.
  10. Click panel composer. Type "Shorten the intro".
  11. Click Send. Tool "Update article"; the intro paragraph swaps to the
      one-sentence version ON SCREEN. Hold 2s.

BEAT 4 — Creative into the library (VO 07)
  12. Click rail back arrow. ONLY the rail returns to the main menu;
      editor content stays put.
  13. Click rail "Ad Creatives" -> content: creatives library (masonry).
  14. Click panel composer. Type "Make an autumn ad".
  15. Click Send. Tool "Generate ad creative"; creative appears in the
      panel thread AND a new tile lands first in the masonry.

BEAT 5 — Schedule it (VO 08)
  16. Click rail "Schedules" -> content: schedules list (no recap row yet).
  17. Click panel composer. Type "Send a performance recap every Monday".
  18. Click Send. Tool "Create schedule"; answer confirms Mondays 9:00 —
      and THEN the "Weekly performance recap" row appears at the top of
      the list, after the agent creates it.

BEAT 6 — Close (VO 09)
  19. Click rail "Integrations" -> integrations page under the close line:
      "Everything you've connected, it sees. Everything you see, it can
      change." Outro lockup.
