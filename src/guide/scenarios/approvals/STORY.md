# guide-approvals

Hook: your marketing has a to-do list that writes itself — and does itself.
Setup: Approvals page — while you were busy, the agent's scheduled runs went through your ads and your site and filed everything worth fixing. Each card = one concrete change, waiting for one click.
Work: open a paid-ads card → detail sheet: Evidence (real numbers), the exact Before/After, Expected impact — you judge with full context, not blind trust. Approve → the agent goes into Google Ads and applies it itself.
Teach: Reject is not a dead end — you say why, and the agent proposes better next time. Every decision teaches it your taste: the more you decide, the closer its proposals get to what you'd do yourself. Blocked cards = the agent is honest when it lacks access, and tells you exactly what to connect.
Close: check the queue over coffee, click what you agree with — the agent does the hands work. That's marketing on autopilot with you in charge.

Click flow:
1. nav Approvals (badge 9) → Pending list
2. click paid-ads card → detail sheet (Evidence / Before-After / Impact)
3. sheet Approve → status flips to Applying → Applied
4. click redirect-chain SEO card → sheet → Approve (second proof, faster)
5. click Reject on a weak card → reject dialog → type reason → Reject
6. blocked card: Blocked pill, "Remove blocker" — agent says what to connect
7. tab History → resolved list (Applied/Rejected)
8. close
