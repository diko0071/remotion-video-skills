# guide-blog-studio

Hook: no CMS? No developer? Your blog can live on Ryze — designed in your style.
Setup: Blog Studio — live preview of YOUR hosted blog (real article cards) + design panel. Hosted free on a Ryze subdomain, or on your own domain (chip in the topbar).
Work: click a theme tile → Night → the whole blog flips dark; the panel holds colors, fonts, corners, layout — design without a developer.
Wow: "Match my website style" → the agent reads your live site and rebuilds the blog's colors, fonts, header and footer to match.
Sitemaps/Google: Search Console section — verify once on a custom domain, Ryze submits the sitemap and pings Google on every publish; put one link to the blog in your site nav so it crawls fast.
Close: articles from your content plan publish straight here — a full blog with zero infrastructure.

Clicks: theme Night → theme Warm back? (keep Night → wow) → Match (press) → close. SEO section shown on-screen while narrated.
