# guide-visibility

Education: what AI visibility (GEO) is, how it differs from SEO, why prompts
matter, how to add them, and how to read the GEO dashboard.
Sources: seo-audit app/modules/seo/queries (content.queries.ts) +
app/modules/analytics (content.analytics.ts geo section). ~2.5-3 min.

Click path:
1. GEO dashboard (Dashboard -> GEO tab already active): hero — AI Share of
   Voice, your rank among brands named in answers; leaderboard.
2. Visibility Over Time + Visibility by Assistant — reading the charts.
3. Prompts table on the dashboard — what a prompt is.
4. Queries page -> Prompts tab: the tracked list.
5. Track prompts dialog: "One prompt per line — questions your buyers ask AI
   assistants. Each prompt is checked on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and
   Perplexity." Add two prompts -> added.
6. Re-ask dialog: "Run prompts" — all assistants re-asked, dashboard updates.
7. Rankings + Top Domains beat: who gets named, which sites get cited.
8. Close + lockup.

Wow: seeing who ChatGPT names INSTEAD of you — and turning that into a list
you control.
Difference vs SEO (VO): SEO is where you rank on Google; GEO is whether AI
assistants name you when buyers ask them directly.
