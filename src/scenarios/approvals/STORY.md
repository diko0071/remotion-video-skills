# approvals — "You approve. Ryze ships."

Voiced promo (~45s, wide) for Approvals: the agent reads the whole account
continuously, and the only thing left for the marketer is a click. The
paradigm shift is the point — you no longer go into a chat to ask; the work
arrives in Slack or your inbox, already decided, already built.

Arc:
1. Hook (beats) — nobody has opened this account in six days, and it's never
   performed better.
2. Data torrent (new mechanic) — a wall of rows (campaigns, creatives, days
   of history) streams past, counter climbs past 200,000, camera pulls back
   to show it is endless, then the whole wall COLLAPSES into one finding line.
3. It arrives — Slack message from Ryze ("3 approvals waiting"), cut to an
   email of the same thing. You never went looking.
4. One click each — two approval cards: move budget to the winner, pause the
   loser. Cursor approves, card stamps and leaves, the live account reacts.
5. The opening — the payoff approval: a whole new campaign with the creatives
   ALREADY generated, budget and audience filled in. One approve, it goes live.
6. The curve — ROAS climbs across weeks, "no login" marks under the axis.
7. Punch — "You approve. Ryze ships." → lockup.

Kit: kinetic-beats, slack-ui, stage camera + cursor, monochrome status
language (dot + text, numbers typographic, no coloured pills).
New: DataTorrent (rows → collapse into one finding), ApprovalCard.
