# claude-directory (Ryze is in the Claude Directory)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, 720 frames (24s), 120 BPM. Announcement promo in the
mcp-hook dynamics: capsule with overshoot and layered pops, whip-zoom with
directional blur, one object carries every cut, push-only camera, holds that
breathe, an endcard whose letters cool from coral to white.

Hook (0-1s): "Your marketing autopilot" rises letter by letter, slides left
with x-blur and is gone before the capsule lands.
Setup (1-3.9s): a black capsule rises from below with an overshoot; inside it
the real Claude Directory page (dark) assembles in layers: title, subtitle,
search, filters; the cursor types "ryze", the Ryze AI card pops with its
Community pill and green check; whip-zoom onto the card; click.
Entrance (3.9-6.3s): hard cut to light Claude (kit, 1.6x). The Ryze icon is
carried from the card's projected position into the composer as a chip. The
prompt types "Run my marketing this week.", the send button breathes, press.
Work (6.3-20s): five blocks, hard cuts between, push-only camera inside:
paid ads (campaign card, ROAS ticking), creatives (six creatives pop into a
grid), dashboards (line chart draws, stat cards count), SEO fixes (rows flip
to green checks, score ring 31 -> 92), audits (checks machine-gun with an
accelerating cascade, health score). Every tool row carries the Ryze favicon.
Wow (20-22.4s): cut to white; five result cards fly onto an orbit around the
Claude star, tilt -4deg, the orbit keeps turning.
Close (22.4-24s): the ground inverts in six frames; "Ryze. Now in the Claude
Directory." rises; a pill types claude.ai/directory coral, cooling to white.
