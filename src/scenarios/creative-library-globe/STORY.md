# creative-library-globe

Hook: one winning creative fills the frame; the camera pulls back slow-slow-
slow-slow, then snaps — the tile is one card in a spinning globe of 900
winning creatives (the real creative-library globe view).
Problem: none stated — the globe itself is the wow, no setup needed.
Work-on-screen: four creatives peel off the globe and fly right, camera pans
with them, they land in the search bar; "recreate for my brand" types in,
cursor clicks Recreate for your brand.
Wow: the slow-then-snap reveal of the globe, and the fly-right-land-in-bar
continuity (one gesture, no cuts).
Close: "100,000 creatives. Ready to reuse. Created for you." + Ryze lockup.

Scene budget (30fps): zoom+reveal ~4.7s, fly+land+type+click ~4s, text ~4.3s,
lockup tail. Target ~15-16s core + lockup.
