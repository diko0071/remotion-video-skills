# backlink-exchange

Voiced promo, 30s, VO-beat language.

Hook: "Still paying $200... per backlink?" — kinetic, money pain.
Problem: agencies charge per link, plus weeks of outreach (invoice plates).
Turn: "So we built something no agency can: a network of 3,000 stores" —
the web mechanic reused: store cards with DR badges, 2,500 of them in the
exchange.
Work-on-screen (wow, new mechanic): live link-swap — two real articles side
by side, an anchor highlights inside the left text and a strand flies to the
right store, mirrored back; do-follow badges land.
Scale: counter 50 backlinks / month.
Punch: "$10,000 worth of links. Included." — Included huge with marker+sparks.
Close: "Backlink Exchange" title beat -> Ryze lockup.
