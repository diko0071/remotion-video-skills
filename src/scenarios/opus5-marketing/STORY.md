# opus5-marketing ("Opus 5 for Marketing")

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, 120 BPM (15f/beat, 60f/bar), ~41s, music only, all copy kinetic.
Built on the Aside launch (references/opus5-marketing), with the model-chip push from
claude-scene, the labelled cases of Helena (seijin), results type from the inDrive case,
and the Claude + Ryze scene grammar of claude-directory / mcp-hook.

Hook (0-2.2s, dark): "AI writes your ads." settles, "Then it stops." under it.
Problem (2.2-7s, dark): a dark chat, "Launch my spring campaign on Meta." and four refusals
stack ("I can't access your ad account" ...), "Why did you stop?", the refusals peel off
one by one into a trash can.
Reveal (7-11s): "Introducing" rises and leaves, a warm light curtain grows from the floor,
"Opus 5" rises huge with a glow, "for Marketing", "with Ryze AI".
Work (11-25s, Claude light): welcome, whip push onto the "Opus 5 High" model chip, cut to the
composer, the prompt types, send breathes and is pressed. Thread: five Ryze tool rows, spark
status, the answer streams, six creatives pop, the campaign card appears, the cursor presses
Launch, it flips to Live. "Wait, it actually launched it?" / "Yes. It's live on Meta..."
Breadth (25-33s): three labelled cases, each tighter: Ad spend spike / Competitor launch /
Traffic drop; agent message, one-word reply, green Done line.
Proof (33-37s, dark): "5,000+" counts up, "marketers already run on it"; "Thousands of ads /
live every week."
Close (37-41s): Ryze AI lockup, tagline "Opus 5 for Marketing".
