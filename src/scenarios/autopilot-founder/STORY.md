# autopilot-founder

Measured replica of the BabyLoveGrowth stick-figure ad
(`references/fb-1827384028291239/`, 36s vertical), re-skinned for Ryze.

Hook: "If you're a founder getting zero traffic..." — shrug figure, label
"Founder" with a hand-drawn arrow, then slumped at the laptop.
Problem: one small figure holding its head while three demands land on it
with arrows: Fresh content / Backlinks / AI Visibility. Then question marks
at the monitor, then money flying out of the pockets.
Turn: Ryze AI logo over the figure with an AUTOPILOT badge; feet up on the
desk; paste the URL once.
Work-on-screen: thumbs up while three stacked benefits land at the top with
a circled check for each (daily SEO articles / backlinks / AI visibility).
Life: walks away from the desk, sleeps, checks the phone (traffic up), a
clock races while he cheers.
Close: 3,000+ websites crowd, then TRY / Ryze AI / FREE.

Mechanic: one word at a time in the accent colour under the figure, synced
to the VO word marks (whole take transcribed, `vo-marks.json` `_take`).
Character: one generated stick figure (Gemini, `public/autopilot-founder/`),
every pose generated from the base image as a reference, white keyed to alpha.
