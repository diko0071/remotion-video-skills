# creative-library-globe-avalanche

Same film as creative-library-globe through the send click, then the payoff
swaps: instead of text beats, winning creatives slam onto a centre stack with
alternating slight tilts, accelerating 1-2-then-machine-gun to 10. Text lands
over the pile: "100,000 winners for your brand." then "Ready to be recreated."
while more creatives keep dropping on top of the words. Dark background start
to finish; dark Ryze lockup closes.
