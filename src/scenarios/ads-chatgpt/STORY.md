# ads-chatgpt

Hook: "Launch ads in [ChatGPT]" — the first AI ad manager for ChatGPT.
Problem: a luggage brand has winning photos but no way into ChatGPT ads.
Work-on-screen: three Away brand photos fan in an arc, fly into the composer,
the cursor opens the plus menu and toggles ChatGPT on, "Create and launch my
ads in ChatGPT." is typed and sent — the agent generates three new creatives,
builds the campaign, and shows the ready card: "Everything's ready — click
Create". The cursor clicks Create.
Wow: the campaign card flipping to live after one click.
Close: "The first AI ad manager for [ChatGPT]" and the Ryze lockup.
