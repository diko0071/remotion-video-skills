# sell-agents ("Sell what AI agents want")

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, 120 BPM, ~27s, music only. Storytelling and lines from the
launches prototype (launches-sandy.vercel.app #l1); the look is ours: chrome-ext and
mcp-hook language (Outfit headlines with a coral accent, inline objects in the line,
real Chrome with a real site, the real Ryze Technical Audit page, directional blur on
every fast move, hard cuts on the bar, an alive URL endcard).

Hook (0-4.6s): "Your customer in 2025" with the photo of a person inline; the last
digit rolls to 2026 and the photo flips into an exploded GPU; "Sell what AI agents
want." rises under it; the lines whip out left.
Swarm (4.6-7.4s): a live store in Chrome; ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Grok
tiles fly in from every edge and land on the page faster and faster; a chip counts the
share of AI-agent visits.
Share (7.4-10.6s): "[50%] of your traffic is already AI agents." with the agent logos
popping inline; 50% rolls to 90%+ and the second line swaps to "in a few months."
Checklist (10.6-16.8s): "But how do you sell to an agent?" settles and whips out; 38
items fly in from every edge onto a tilted wall, faster and faster; "100+ things agents
look for." pops dark over it; the wall is sucked into it.
Connect (16.8-18.6s): "Connect your site to Ryze. It does all of it."
Fix (18.6-25.4s): the real Technical Audit page; the cursor presses Fix with agent; the
LLM score climbs 57 -> 96 and the health gauge 62 -> 94; the site-wide agent checks
tick one by one, the site score goes to 100.
Close (25.4-29s): alive endcard, "Ryze." coral, "Sell what AI agents want.", the pill
types ryze.ai/agents coral cooling to white.
