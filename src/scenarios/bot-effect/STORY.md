# bot-effect (replica of x.com/bot "now available on Android")

Wide 1920x1080, 300 frames, frame-accurate copy of
`references/bot-effect/source.mp4`. Study replica: the reference's own audio
track is muxed on delivery.

Hook: a Pixel lockscreen on white with two notifications.
Setup: the camera dives INTO the screen; the wallpaper becomes the stage and
the Grok notification is gone, only Benji's card remains.
Work-on-screen: the Grok Bot notification arrives from above and pushes Benji
down a slot, seen at 1.5x.
Wow: the camera breathes back out; the clip peels off the white with a lag and
the phone lands on its exact opening framing.
Close: white, "Grok Bot is now available on Android" word by word with the
Android head; the Grok mark pops, shrinks, slides left, "Grok Bot" pops in.

Shot list and every measured frame: `references/bot-effect/BREAKDOWN.md`.
