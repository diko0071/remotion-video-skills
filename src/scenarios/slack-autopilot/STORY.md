# slack-autopilot

Hook: "Your marketing runs itself. Now in Slack."
Problem: running ads means living in dashboards you never wanted to open.
Work-on-screen: a Ryze approval lands in #marketing — a leaking Meta ad set
with Approve/Skip buttons. The cursor clicks Approve, Ryze replies done, and
a cut to the platform shows the ad set flip to Paused with the spend saved.
A second beat drops the weekly performance report straight into the channel.
Wow: a real marketing decision made with one click inside Slack.
Close: "Approvals. Reports. In Slack." and the Ryze lockup.
