# deck-gen

Hook: "Client decks take days. Yours takes one prompt." — kinetic, accent on
"one prompt".
Problem: beautiful client-ready decks are slow, manual, designer-bound.
Work-on-screen: data-source chips (GA4, GSC, Meta) fly into the composer, one
prompt is typed, the agent reads revenue/search/channel data and designs the
deck — a cover preview pops in chat and MORPHS into a full-bleed slide, then
the whole deck machine-guns through: chart slides, forecast, decay analysis,
plan — accelerating.
Wow: the morph into the editorial cover ("+38%" black split, serif type) and
the accelerating slide run — every slide is a REAL rendered template.
Close: a masonry wall of all 9 decks + 5 live dashboards ("Decks.
Dashboards. Reports."), a dashboard camera-walk, Ryze lockup.
