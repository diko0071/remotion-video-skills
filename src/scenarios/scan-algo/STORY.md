# scan-algo — full script (v2, screen-by-screen)

## Laws this script obeys
1. Every frame is a continuation of the previous frame. Nothing appears from
   nowhere: a screen enters because the camera moved to it, because it grew
   out of a click, or because the previous object transformed into it.
2. The scan shell (dark rail left + paper right) is ONE mounted frame for the
   whole audit. The rail ticks checks; only the paper content changes. The
   rail is the thread the viewer holds through all eight screens.
3. Transitions inside the paper: zoom-into-a-tile, content slide on the beat,
   or annotation drawn over what is already there. Never a hard swap of the
   whole stage.
4. Every claim on screen is the real product's claim (copy from
   content.seo-audit.ts), every number consistent across screens.
5. The tilt is not a trick: it happens once, at the very end, as the camera
   pulls back from the finished verdict — the screen recedes into the CTA.

### ACT 3 — the audit, screen by screen (NEW — the meat)
Each check: rail row activates with shimmer -> paper content plays ->
sublog values land in the rail -> rail row flips to a green check ON the
beat -> paper content for the next check enters.

8. 0:16 WEBSITE — the wall of 20 page tiles cascades in (screenshots +
   skeletons). Counter "24 of 24 pages". Camera drifts along the wall.
9. 0:20 Camera ZOOMS INTO the home tile until it is full-frame (the tile IS
   the next screen — continuation). Red annotation boxes draw over the page:
   "Missing meta description", "H1 missing", "31 images without alt text".
10. 0:24 SPEED — the annotated page slides left and shrinks into a phone
    mockup; next to it the speed gauge draws: needle sweeps to 6.8s, past
    the 2.5s redline. Sublog: "Mobile load 6.8s" red.
11. 0:28 CATALOG — "Your catalog, product by product": product cards
    (sizzle, drizzle, frizzle, trio...) slide in as a row; chips stamp onto
    them: "Thin description", "3 images missing alt text". Analyzing pulse
    on each card as it is inspected.
12. 0:33 GOOGLE — a real Google SERP window slides up over the paper (still
    inside the shell): query "best olive oil for cooking" types into the
    search bar, results render, the page auto-scrolls down... and down...
    stamp slams: "NOT IN TOP 50". Second query "olive oil gift set" —
    scroll stops at position #7, badge "#7 · YOU". Sublog collects: two
    misses, one #7.
13. 0:40 AI — "Do the AIs recommend you?": four engine cards (ChatGPT,
    Claude, Perplexity, Gemini — real icons, real model names) type their
    answers in parallel; stamps land one by one: "Mentioned" (1) /
    "grazaoliveoil.com — not mentioned" (3).
14. 0:46 COMPETITORS — "Who owns your market": bubble chart inflates,
    rival bubbles grow big, your bubble stays small with "— you" label.
15. 0:50 REVENUE — "What this costs you": money counter spins up
    ($6,400/mo -> $76,800/yr) while breakdown rows land: searches where
    rivals take the click, slow pages, catalog gaps, AI answers that skip
    you. This is the gut-punch screen; hold it a beat longer.
16. 0:55 VERDICT — rail flips its last check; paper clears into "Your
    website's search health": score ring draws to 34, band "Poor", the four
    issue lines from the audit stack under it. Everything the viewer just
    watched, in one card.

### ACT 4 — close
17. 0:59 Camera pulls back from the verdict; the whole shell recedes and

    aura behind.
18. 1:01 "Google moved. Find out what it cost you."
19. 1:03 Lockup: Ryze AI — "Scan your site free at ryze.ai".

## Owner comparison (what they do that v1 missed)
- Owner shows EVERY analysis stage as its own surface (site, reviews, FB
  posts, menu, Google Business) with agent pills crawling over them — my v1
  showed one surface for 8 seconds. v2 gives each check its own surface.
- Owner's transitions are continuations: map -> zoom into tile -> tile
  becomes the surface -> annotations draw on it. v2 copies this exact
  grammar (wall -> zoom into tile -> annotate).
- Owner's score/issues/$ counters GROW as the analysis proceeds; v2 moves
  money to its own screen with a spinning counter and lands the score last.
- Owner's tilt+aura moment belongs to the FIX phase; ours closes the film —
  but it is ramped out of a camera pull-back, never a jump.
- Owner is 110s with a founder on camera; we have no footage, so our film
  is 75s of pure UI — pacing compensates: no state parked over 3s except
  the revenue screen (deliberate).
