# done-for-you (SEO and GEO actually done for you: it audits and fixes everything)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, ~29s. Built from the kit the way launch-ads and
fix-seo are: scan scene -> input scene (minimize-into-input) -> four
ChatScenes on one conversation (history settled, continuationMarks) ->
preview morphs to full stage (ExpandToStage) -> rise-letters endcard.

Laws: one conversation, history never re-animates; every result lives in the
assistant message; every click has a SceneCursor on a data-click target;
camera = CameraRig shots on objects, never coordinates.

Hook (0-3.3s): the client site in a browser card, two scan beams, three
score cards punch in: SEO 23, GEO 12, Health 41; camera pushes onto them.
Setup (3.3-7.3s): the site shrinks into the composer as a chip, "Fix
everything." types, cursor presses send.
Work 1 (7.3-13.6s): the prompt with the site chip, "Found 48 issues across
your site. Fixing all of them now -", five tool rows cascade faster and
faster, then a preview widget: the old site wipes into the redesigned one
under a beam, SEO ring counts 23 -> 71.
Work 2 (13.6-18.6s): the agent continues: "Site fixed. 12 publications in the
exchange match your niche -", a Backlink Exchange card with a Find placements
button; cursor click; a grid of twelve publishers pops, chain arrows flow left
to right, do-follow checks land in a cascade.
Work 3 (18.6-22.3s): "Writing 30 articles for the keywords you don't rank for
yet -", an article card, then article cards machine-gun into a two-column pile
with Published pills.
Wow (22.3-27s): "Done. Here is where you stand now -", a site preview in the
message morphs into the full stage (same geometry as the opening), three
green score cards punch 92 / 84 / 96.
Close (27-29.4s): the ground inverts, "No more engineers." / "No more
freelancers." rise letter by letter, a pill types get-ryze.ai.

## Clicks

1. input: cursor to composer.send, press at SEND (the chip is already seated).
2. links: cursor to widget.submit (Find placements), press at FIND_AT; the
   placements grid mounts 8 frames later.
