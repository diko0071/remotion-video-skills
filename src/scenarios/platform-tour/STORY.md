# platform-tour

- **Hook (0-3s)** — the Ryze app, rail open, cursor lands on Home. This is the whole product, one take.
- **Problem** — a marketing stack is normally ten tools; here every surface lives behind one rail.
- **Work on screen** — the cursor walks the rail top to bottom: Home, Approvals, Chats, Schedules,
  Templates, Brand, Reports (into a report), Integrations, then the SEO product (Home, Content Plan
  list → calendar, Technical Audit, Backlinks, AI Search, Blog Studio) and Paid Ads (Dashboard,
  Creatives, Competitor Ads, Ad Templates), closing on Usage.
- **Wow** — no dead screen: every click lands on a page already full of a real workspace's data.
- **Close** — back to Home, rail highlighted, the whole platform seen in under a minute.

Purpose: coverage QA — one continuous take that proves every ported page renders.
