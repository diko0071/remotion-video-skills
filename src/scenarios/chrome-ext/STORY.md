# chrome-ext (Ryze AI Chrome extension: one-click AI visibility)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, ~30s, Outfit headlines on white (Gumloop language:
hard cuts, one thought per screen, the panel appears only to catch a result).
Real Chrome (kit/chrome-ui), real site screenshots (Browserbase, ignored in
git), the panel is kit/ext-ui, a 1:1 port of the extension's ui.css; every
number is the live extension backend response (data.json, sites.json).

Open (0-4.7s): notion.so in Chrome, the badge pops "93", the camera flies to
the toolbar icon (clamped to the window edge), the cursor clicks, the panel is
born from the icon and grows until it covers the frame; on the dark ground:
INTRODUCING / One-click AI visibility. Hard cut.
Overview (4.7-13s): ChatGPT answers to real questions stream big, each cites a
notion.so page as a pill; pills lift to the corners of the frame, then shoot
into the centre and the GEO ring pops and counts to 30; the panel rises from
below, the ring lands in its gauge slot, rows land. Hold, cut.
Sources (13-20.5s): "Who AI names next to [N] it." with the Notion tile born
inside the line; the line exits, the tile grows to the centre, six cited
domains fly in from different edges onto a slowly rotating circle joined by
arcs; the circle spins up, the tiles fly into their list rows and the tile
into the hero card as the panel rises on the Sources tab; the cursor clicks
Overview; the panel zooms out to its real dock over the site.
Any site (20.5-26.5s): behind the docked panel seventeen sites slide in faster
and faster (14 -> 3 frames), only the numbers in the panel change; the last
frame slides out left while the gauge rides right into the new scene and
lands inline in "One click to check anyone's [73] AI visibility."
Close: the Ryze AI lockup, 112 frames.

## Clicks

1. CLICK (f84): SceneCursor on `chrome.ext` (toolbar icon), press dip, mouse-click sfx.
2. Sources: cursor from below-right to the Overview tab (analytic position, panel scale 1.4), press at SR.overviewAt, mouse-click sfx.
