# competitor-ads — "Track your competitors' ads"

Voiced promo (~40s, wide) for the Competitor Ads feature: track a competitor
by domain, Ryze watches their live Google/Meta ads 24/7, notifies on new
launches, collects everything into a filterable library (industry / platform /
duration / trending), and the agent remakes their winners for your brand.

Arc:
1. Hook (beats) — "Your competitors are running ads right now. Have you seen them?"
2. Track (composer + chat) — type `ritual.com`, send, agent confirms tracking.
3. Radar (new primitive) — your site in the center, competitors on elliptic
   orbits, a rotating sweep beam pings each one; "N active ads" badges.
4. Notify — beam catches a launch, notification card drops in, cursor clicks,
   the new creative expands ("Launched today · Still running").
5. Library — real product UI: header, brand chips, filter bar. Cursor filters
   by industry → platform → 90+ days; the wall reflows live; long-runners
   stay as "their winners".
6. Copy — winner flies into the composer, "Make this ad for my brand.",
   agent returns 3 versions in your style (ask-creatives primitive).
7. Punch — "Their best ads are your starting point." → Try Competitor Ads → lockup.

Assets: public/creative-wall (real ads with running-days in filename),
public/brands + public/stores favicons. VO: eleven_v3, word marks via scribe.
