# o-night-shift — "Your dot's night shift" (launch #2 for ChatGPT dots, ryze.ai/gpt)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, 21.2s (636 frames), 120 BPM, music only.
Claim: with Ryze, your ChatGPT dot runs your marketing while you sleep.
Grammar borrowed from the Grok Bot "Team Bots" film (references/bot-2104661562715967548):
one anchor message per world, real artefacts assembling around it, a colour per chapter,
short copy with numbers. Story, palette, characters and transitions are ours.

## Laws
- The lime clock plate is the one object that lives through the whole film. It rolls to a
  new time on every cut and at the end becomes the URL plate.
- The dot's note card is the anchor of every night world. It never moves; its text swaps on the
  cut, the evidence assembles around it and leaves before the next cut.
- Every change has a cause on screen: a switch flips, a line strikes through, a 404 chip
  turns Live, the cursor clicks Approve.
- Night is a dark ground tinted by the channel; morning is the cream launch-kit ground.

## Script
1. 0-90 Tonight. Clock 11:52 PM. Composer "Message your dot": types "Watch our marketing
   tonight?". Cursor clicks send (click sfx). The user bubble appears, the dot (the white
   satin ball) turns ^ ^ and hops; its reply: "On it. Get some sleep."
2. 90-195 1:14 AM, Meta (deep blue). Note from "Your dot": "Cost per purchase jumped 38% on 3 ad sets.
   Paused them." Around it: Meta tile, CPA card $80 with a red spike, the Ad sets card
   with three rows whose switches flip off one by one, two creatives, the blue Meta ball
   x x → ^ ^.
3. 195-285 3:40 AM, Google Ads (deep green). Note: "14 search terms spent $410 with zero
   sales. Blocked them." Search terms card: four terms strike through and get a Negative
   tag; wasted spend card $410/day → $0/day; the green ball > < → ^ ^.
4. 285-360 5:05 AM, Shopify (deep purple). Note: "37 product pages were showing 404.
   Fixed the links." Four product cards flip 404 → Live, "+33 more"; the purple ball o o → ^ ^.
5. 360-420 Machine-gun, 20 frames each: 5:41 AM GA4 "Fixed purchase tracking.", 6:12 AM
   TikTok "Shipped 5 new creatives.", 6:55 AM Search Console "Rewrote 6 slipping pages."
   Each with its ball popping in happy.
6. 420-546 Sunrise. 7:58 AM. The dot alone in the dark. The cream Ryze sun rises from the bottom
   and floods the frame: morning. The camera pushes in while the dot shrinks into the avatar of its
   morning card:
   "Good morning. Here's your night." 3 ad sets paused / $410/day saved / 37 pages fixed.
   "Move $200/day to Retarget · 7 days? ROAS 4.1" — cursor clicks Approve (click sfx),
   the button turns green Approved, the dot winks.
7. 546-636 End. The clock plate flies to the centre and rolls from 7:58 AM to "ryze ai/gpt",
   "Let your dot run your marketing:" rises above it, and the dot springs up into the gap as
   the dot of "ryze.ai".
