# walkthrough-paid-ads

- **Hook** — one chat box runs your ads; the narrator explains it like to someone who has never used an ad platform.
- **Problem** — ad tools assume you know what CPA, ROAS and ad groups are. This one does not.
- **Work on screen** — ask in plain English → creatives generated, a chart answered, a fix proposed and approved; then the dashboard, campaigns, creatives, portfolio, library, competitor ads, schedules.
- **Wow** — nothing touches money without your approval, and it keeps working on a schedule.
- **Close** — back on the dashboard: you ask, it works, you approve.

Voice: ElevenLabs "River" (calm, informative), music sits far under the narration.
