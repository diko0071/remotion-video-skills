# veed-launch (1:1 replica of VEED "one prompt, ready-to-post video", LinkedIn)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps. Measured replica of `references/lnkd-creating/source.mp4`
(47s, no voiceover, music only). Brand is NOT adapted: same palette (white,
#121212, acid green), same copy, same beats. Our ad creatives fill the cards
where the reference shows talking heads. Built in parts.

Part 1 (0-8.3s): a torus of ~40 cards grows from a point and spins; the camera
pushes into the hole; "The / future / of / video creation" one word at a time
inside the hole; the ring implodes; a stack of plates (dotted card, white card,
green plate, white box) pops with "IS HERE" over two crossfading creatives,
then shrinks to a point; dark: "Welcome" (italic flash, then regular), "to",
"VEED" with a mosaic of echoing tiles bursting around it and a white grid
tile, then the mosaic collapses.
Part 2 (8.3-14.2s): "start with a template", the 3D fan of vertical cards
with the cursor, "or just type", the giant caret, the typed prompt.
Part 3 (14.2-23.6s): Character / Music / Subtitles grids, chips fly into the
prompt card, the script streams, Generate, dot-matrix dissolve into the result.
Part 4 (23.6-39.2s): From your head to the feed, panels, phone tilt, camera
app, clones, pick one, the row of four.
Part 5 (39.2-47s): kinetic close and the green endcard.
