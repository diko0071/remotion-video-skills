# o-marketing — "Your marketing stack meets your dot" (ChatGPT dots launch, ryze.ai/gpt)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps, 22s (660 frames), music only. Launches with OpenAI DevDay: Ryze is
the first platform that connects your ChatGPT dot (OpenAI's always-on agent, launched at
DevDay 2026-09-29) to your ads, SEO and store.

Idea: your marketing stack is a crowd of DevDay-style characters in daily panic. The dot arrives,
Ryze plugs the dot into every one of them, and the panic turns into calm. Emotion is carried by
the characters' glyph eyes; the look is the Ryze launch kit (the #16 / #8 family).

Cast: the dot is the light satin ball with star eyes. The tools are coloured satin balls, each
with a white logo badge: Meta Ads (blue, x x), Google Ads (green, > <), Shopify (purple,
o o), GA4 (orange, - -), TikTok Ads (dark, white x x), Search Console (sky, > <).
Fixed: ^ ^. Balls are gpt-image renders on transparent ground (scripts/gen-o-balls.ts ->
public/glyph-balls/), eyes are SVG glyphs drawn over them.

## Beats
1. 0-100 Panic close-ups, whip to whip. Ball left, LaunchCard right with a red metric,
   sparkline and chip, everything shaking: Meta "$80 cost per purchase, ▲ 38% overnight",
   Google Ads "$410/day wasted, 14 search terms, 0 sales", Shopify "37 broken",
   GA4 "0 purchases tracked". "Your marketing stack, every morning."
2. 100-225 Chaos wide: all six jitter under red pills. The dot drops in at 150, lands at 162 with
   a ripple and a camera shake; everyone freezes and turns to the dot; push in on the stare.
   "Then your dot showed up."
3. 225-270 The dot close-up: the Ryze sun badge flies in and slaps onto the dot at 244, a lime ring
   pulses, the eyes become + +.
4. 270-330 Lines shoot from the dot to every tool, each tool pings and looks up (o o).
   "Ryze connects it to every tool."
5. 330-420 Fix close-ups: a lime pulse runs down the line, the card tweens to green
   (Meta $80 -> $31 "▼ 61% · 3 ad sets paused", Google Ads $410 -> $0/day
   "14 negative keywords added", Shopify "37 fixed"), happy jump, confetti.
   "It fixes them, tool by tool."
6. 420-570 Wide again: GA4, TikTok and Search Console flip one after another, the dot smiles.
   The tools glide into a ring around the dot, hop twice together, the dot winks.
   "Your stack, finally calm."
7. 570-660 End card: "Let your dot run your marketing:" and ryze.ai/gpt typed on a lime plate,
   a row of all seven balls under it, the dot winks again.

## Laws
- Launch kit look: cream #FDFAF3 ground, Plus Jakarta headlines with lime #B6EE3A plates,
  Ryze corner lockup that pulses on the slap.
- Close-ups are joined by whips with directional blur; the wide crowd is entered by hard
  cuts. Inside a crowd shot the KeyedRig zoom never decreases.
- Every change has a cause on screen: a landing, a slap, a line, a pulse.
- The end-card text starts on the cut frame, never over the previous shot.
