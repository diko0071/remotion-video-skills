# superagent (replica of Crowdreply "Introducing SuperAgent", Ryze skin)

Wide 1920x1080, 30fps. Measured replica of
`references/crowdreply-superagent/source.mp4` (53s). Built in parts.

Hook (0-5.4s): one line types itself letter by letter — "What if getting
found in AI answers was as simple as asking?" — growing from the centre,
then sliding left with its right edge pinned; "AI" is a coral serif twice
the size with a chromatic pop; a magnifier rises from below onto "AI" and
inside the glass the word cycles into Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and OpenAI
tiles; the lens drops away down-left and the line whips so that "was as
simple as asking?" lands centred.
Setup (5.4-14.5s): the dashboard assembles from falling widgets, wraps into
a browser window, tilts, more cards fly in, the camera flies to the close
button and the cursor clicks it.
Work (14.5-40s): "Now / You / Just / Ask" on drifting colour, the Ask button
morphs into a glowing app icon, "Introducing Ryze SuperAgent", the agent
chat: greeting, prompt, answer, three gap cards, Initiating Actions, score.
Wow (40-47s): feature cards fly onto the gradient, prompt chips scatter
around the orb.
Close (47-53s): dark pill typing get-ryze.ai/agent, "Try it free", lockup.
