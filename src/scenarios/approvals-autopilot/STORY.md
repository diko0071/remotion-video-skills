- **Wow (24-28s):** "Applied while you slept." — avalanche of applied-fix
  rows stacking with accelerating rhythm, camera pulls back, ends on
  "$1,412/mo recovered".
- **Close (28-36s):** giant "Autopilot." with the switch flipping ON →
  outro: logo, "You approve. Ryze ships.", get-ryze.ai.
