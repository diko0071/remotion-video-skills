# shipper-2 (replica of x.com/shipper_now "Introducing Claude for business-building")

Wide 1920x1080, 490 frames, study replica of `references/shipper-2/source.mp4`
(their audio muxed on delivery, their two footage clips cut from the source).

Hook: "Introducing" resolves out of scrambled glyphs; a Claude chip pops; "for"
and an orange roller of audiences.
Setup: pixel-corruption eats the frame; "SaaS" split around a meme clip.
Work-on-screen: a composer on a posterized orange fluid types the prompt; the
fluid turns grey and the camera pushes into the green send arrow; click.
Wow: a feature chain the camera walks over an ASCII dither field: Beautiful
Interface, Analytics, Back-End, Media Library, Login, Payments.
Close: crowd footage, then the serif "Shipper" endcard with a glitch.

The texture engine is `kit/dither-field` (WebGL, deterministic per frame):
posterize / dots / ascii / blocks, one generator, four skins.
Shot map with measured seconds: `references/shipper-2/BREAKDOWN.md`.
