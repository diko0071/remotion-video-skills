# grok-plugins

Wide 1920x1080 (square cut later), 128 BPM (14f/beat, 56f/bar), ~25s.
Structure borrowed from two references: treg (the Plugins door into Grok Bot)
and Helena on iMessage (a labelled case per screen: the agent messages first,
you answer with one word, Done). Copy is ours.

Hook: the Grok Bot window floats on the golden cream. The cursor clicks
Plugins, the modal opens, "ryze" types in, the Ryze AI card pops, the cursor
clicks Add, it flips to Added. The modal closes and Ryze AI lands in the
sidebar.
Work-on-screen: six cases, one per screen, hard cuts on the bar. Each screen:
the case label on the left (big, ink), a Grok thread card on the right. Ryze
messages first with a real number, the user answers with one word in a black
bubble, Ryze replies Done and the artifact drops in (creatives, competitor
ads, a fix list, a leaderboard, a published post).
Wow: the artifacts are real (creatives from the bucket, dashboards, decks),
and the cadence tightens case by case.
Close: "Your marketing team. One plugin." kinetic line, then the Ryze AI x
Grok bloub lockup, the bloub blinks twice.

## Clicks

1. f8 cursor moves from the thread to the Plugins row (sidebar bottom).
2. f30 click Plugins: press dip, modal scales in over a dimmed window.
3. f40-f66 "ryze" types into the modal search; f70 the Ryze AI card pops
   into results, Typeform and SonarQube pop after it.
4. f72-f96 cursor travels to Add; f98 click: press, ring, label flips to
   Added with a check.
5. f110-f130 modal scales out, the Ryze AI row drops into the sidebar with a
   blink.
6. f140 hard cut: case 1 (Ad spend). Bot message settled from frame 0; user
   reply pops at +18; Done row + artifact at +34; cut at +72.
7. Cases 2-6 the same, each 6f shorter than the previous.
8. Kinetic line, then lockup.

## Cases (label / bot line / reply / artifact)

- Ad spend / "Meta spend is at $9,540 today, 2.3x your daily cap. ROAS
  dropped to 1.4." / "Pause it" / Done: paused 2 ad sets.
- Creatives / "Summer sale creatives are ready, three variants in your
  brand style." / "Ship them" / three creatives.
- Competitors / "Lululemon launched 4 new Meta ads on your keywords this
  morning." / "Show me" / four competitor ads.
- SEO / "12 pages lost rankings after the update. I have fixes ready." /
  "Fix it" / fix list with checks.
- AI visibility / "ChatGPT now cites you for 4 of your 20 tracked
  prompts." / "Nice" / leaderboard card.
- Blog + email / "Sale announcement post and the email are drafted." /
  "Post it" / Published + Sent.
