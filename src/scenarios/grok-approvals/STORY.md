# grok-approvals

Wide 1920x1080, 128 BPM (14f/beat, 56f/bar), ~21s. Dark ground like grok-plugins.
The approvals film told inside Grok Bot: one mechanism, signal → approval →
Grok → one tap.

Hook: "Approvals. Now in Grok Bot." settle line with the Ryze sun and the
Grok bloub as inline tiles.
Signals: five data sources on the left (Meta Ads, Google Ads, Search
Console, Shopify, GA4) pop in, lines draw to the Ryze sun in the middle and
on to three empty slots on the right. Faint pulses run on every line; three
strong pulses travel source → Ryze → slot and each one pops an approval
card: Move $40/day into Retargeting, Pause ad set CPA 2.4×, Fix 12 pages
that lost rankings.
Carry (the mcp-hook document move): the cursor grabs the stack, the three
cards gather into a pile with a "3" badge, the pile lifts and travels while
the signals page slides up and the Grok Bot window slides in from below.
The pile drops into the Ryze AI thread; "You shared 3 approvals", Ryze
replies "Got them. I will send each one here. One tap to approve."
Wow: the window opens with the thread already anchored to the bottom, the
camera pushes in, and three "Ryze AI wants to run a task" cards spring into
a row under "Yes. I have updates for you." The kit ClickCursor taps Approve
on each, one after another, each flips to "Approved. Running now."
Close: the Ryze AI × Grok bloub lockup, the bloub blinks twice. No text tail.

## Clicks

1. GRAB.at: cursor clicks the top card and sweeps down; cards gather into a pile.
2. CARRY: pile rides the cursor into the Grok Bot icon (CarriedObject), badge 3 with the notify sound.
3. CLICKS[i].at: ClickCursor on ap.i (Approve) for the three cards.
