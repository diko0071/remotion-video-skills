# instinct-calls — "Instinct makes the calls" (launch #8)

Instinct is a personal AI assistant from instinct.com that you text or call.
Ryze connects to it as an MCP connector, so Instinct can watch and change the
ad accounts. The film shows the native iPhone surfaces Instinct lives in:
the incoming call screen with Instinct's contact poster, the in-call screen,
Messages, an outgoing call and the lock screen.

- Hook: an iPhone rings in the middle of the frame, Instinct is calling, rings spread out from it.
- Problem: Meta spend spikes +30% with no extra customers, and that spike is why the phone rings.
- Work on screen: you text "Ask the agency why Google CPA doubled", Instinct dials your agency,
  the pixel-art account manager answers in a sweat.
- Wow: the awkward call happens without you; the answer lands on your lock screen as a text.
- Close: "Try at ryze.ai/instinct".

## Scenes (30 fps, 150 frames each, headline lines fixed)

1. 0-150 "Your ads can call you now." The phone is centred and rings: incoming call from
   Instinct (poster: ink "I" on paper), vibration bursts every second, gold rings spread.
   At 132 the phone glides right to make room, still ringing.
2. 150-300 "It calls when spend spikes." A Meta Ads spend card slides in on the left; the line
   draws, then spikes; "+30%" lands on the tip; a dotted line runs from the tip to the phone
   and the caller subtitle turns into "Spend spike · Meta Ads". A tap answers; live captions
   stream Instinct's words: spend up 30% since noon, no extra customers, pause Retargeting?
3. 300-450 "It can call your agency for you." The phone switches to Messages with Instinct.
   You type "Ask the agency why Google CPA doubled" and send; Instinct replies "On it.
   Calling them now." and the phone dials "Your agency". On the left the pixel-art agency
   office wipes in; a dashed call line runs from the phone to his handset; he answers in a
   sweat: "Uh… we launched broad match on Monday."
4. 450-600 "You get the summary as a text." The call ends; the lock screen (pixel sky) gets a
   notification from Instinct: "Agency: new broad-match campaign. Ryze paused it, CPA back to
   $58." On the left the Google Ads CPA card draws $58, doubles to $116, "Ryze paused it",
   back to $58. From 574 the phone and the card leave downwards.
5. 600-750 End card "Try at ryze.ai/instinct", Ryze logo top-left as in every scene.

## Laws

- Headline and a moving visual from frame 0 of every scene; logo in the same corner always.
- Every change on the phone has a visible cause: a tap, a sent text, a call placed.
- The phone moves in one direction only (centre to right, then out downwards).
