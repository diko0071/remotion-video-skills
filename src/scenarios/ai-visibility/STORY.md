# ai-visibility

- **Hook (0-3s):** the AI Visibility dashboard assembles: gauge counts to a
  red 23, platform cards land with giant ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity logos —
  all INVISIBLE, heatmap fills with misses. You are not in the answers.
- **Problem beat (3-8s):** revenue bars: Yankee $34k, Brooklyn $19k tower
  over your red $2.1k. "You're leaving $8,200/mo on the table."
- **Work on screen (8-19s):** cursor clicks Fix with Agent → agent chat.
  Then the machine gun: ~16 tool calls cascade with tightening gaps
  (schema x24, FAQ hub, rewrites, guides), fix counter races.
- **Wow (19-27s):** back on the dashboard everything flips live: gauge
  re-counts 23 → 61 and turns green, chips INVISIBLE → RANKING, heatmap
  cells flip to green positions, your revenue bar grows to $11.4k.
- **Close (27-33s):** "Be the store AI recommends." → logo, get-ryze.ai.

Mechanics reused: cascade, cursor+sfx, tool-beat rows, beat grid, velocity
blur. New scenario-local: dashboard components (gauge, platform cards,
heatmap, revenue bars) built state-driven so ONE component renders both the
red "before" and green "after".
