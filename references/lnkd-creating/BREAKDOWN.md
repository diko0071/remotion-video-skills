# VEED "one prompt, ready-to-post video" launch (LinkedIn, 47s, 1280x720 @30fps)

Source: https://lnkd.in/p/e2QX9XZT (ugcPost 7497610483991068672). No voiceover,
music only; every word on screen is a kinetic beat at its own timestamp.
Palette: white #FFFFFF, near-black #121212, acid green #A6FF00 (chips, plates,
endcard). Font: a grotesk close to Inter/Helvetica Now; italics for the
secondary word of a pair ("start / with", "a whole / lot").

## Worth taking

- The ring: a torus of cards as the opening object, camera pushing into the hole.
- The plate stack for the headline: three plates sliding from different sides, then the stack shrinks to nothing.
- Chips: each picked option flies from the full-bleed grid into the prompt card as a thumbnail chip.
- Dot-matrix dissolve: halftone dots bleed in, then become the pixels of the result.
- The 3D fan of vertical cards with the cursor pulling one forward.
- Kinetic close: word pairs with italic + accent, a new pair per beat, tight.
