## Why it lands
One object carries the whole opener: the glyph inside the black capsule is
the port, the port becomes the MCP capsule, the capsule becomes the app icon,
the icon gets clicked into Claude. Every camera move is a push; the only
pull-back is masked by the drag. Blur on every fast move.

## What we stole
Capsule-with-port + cable drag as the "connect" gesture; big-type-collapses-
into-line; tile column + strands + pulses as the "all platforms feed one
hub" picture. Ryze skin: gold instead of orange, sun mark as the glyph.
