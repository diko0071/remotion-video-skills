# Selva Gap Map (TikTok, eng, V1) — kinetic typography reference

Source: `source.mp4` (1920x1080, 30fps, 53.4s). Frames at 2fps in `frames/`,
sheets 1-3. NO voiceover — music only; the words carry the narration as
animated on-screen typography. Dmitry's ask: our version DOES use voice, but
keeps this text-with-objects language.

## What makes it work

1. **Kinetic sentences, word-by-word.** Copy types/pops in mid-screen, small
   (~28-40px), centered, on a near-black stage. Key words get color
   (green/blue) the moment they land. Sentences REPLACE each other — one
   thought on screen at a time.
2. **Objects INSIDE the text line.** Emoji-scale objects (eyes 👀, a hammer,
   a magnifier) pop inline between words as they type — the "молоточки и
   глазики" Dmitry means. Then single WORDS blow up huge ("FUTURE" fills the
   frame in mixed cut-out letters) — scale contrast is the rhythm.
3. **A word becomes the next scene.** "FUTURE" morphs: colorful ransom-note
   letters → clean white logo lockup → CTA button. Matter is continuous
   (match-cut thinking, same as our Higgsfield rules).
4. **Content avalanche.** "Upload all the content" → dozens of TikTok video
   cards rain in behind/around the input field, cluttered on purpose,
   labels attached. Sells "we have a LOT" purely by density.
5. **Attribute map.** Text stays center; ~10 small metric chips (Hook
   Velocity, CTA Integration, Brand Encoding…) float scattered around it
   connected by faint lines — a constellation around the claim.
6. **Product payoff.** Dark dashboard cards draw themselves: Market Value
   Curve line chart → THE DATA table overlaying market vs client →
   divergence chips (STRONG DIVERGENCE 2) → recommendation report card
   scrolling. Caption text stays pinned bottom-left while product scrolls.
7. **Close.** "Find out more at cph.byselva.com" → SELVA logo endcard.

## Rules to port (our style, not theirs)

- Keep: kinetic center-stage sentences, inline object pops, one word blown
  huge as a morph carrier, content avalanche for scale claims, caption
  pinned while product scrolls, button-click as scene transition.
- Replace: dark hacker-green theme → our cream/ink promo palette; TikTok
  cards → our ad-templates creatives; их dashboard → наш composer + chat.
- Their pacing is flat-ish; ours must crescendo (house rule).
