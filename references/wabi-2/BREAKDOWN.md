# Wabi — "Introducing Wabi 2.0" (80.5s, 3840x2160, 60fps)

Source: https://x.com/wabi/status/2104721766690222087 (posted 2026-09-28). `source.mp4` here.
Maps: 1 frame per second and dense strips around every transition (scratchpad, 30fps copy).

Calm and premium: white ground, small regular-weight grotesk, words that appear one by one
from grey to ink, a real phone UI kept small in the frame. The brand world (liquid spheres
on a gradient) opens and closes the film, and the logo is born out of it.

## Beats (seconds)
1. 0-1.5 — a glass sphere sits on a glowing curved horizon, purple-orange gradient; the
   camera rises. "Introducing" fades in, then "a new kind of messenger" types with a caret.
2. 5-6.5 — "that makes apps" grows larger in a purple tint; the gradient washes to white.
3. 6.6-9 — "and gets things done", word by word, grey to ink, small, centred. Long hold.
4. 9.1-10 — 3D objects (plane, luggage, disco ball, calendar, ticket, cursor) burst out of
   the centre past the frame edges, leaving one grey chat bubble: "Flight confirmation email
   landed. SFO -> MEX, Sep 30 - Oct 5."
5. 12.6-14 — second bubble ("14 days to go. I've added your flight tickets and a trip
   countdown."), two blurred cards sharpen above it (flight, countdown), and only THEN the
   phone frame fades in around the chat: content first, container after.
6. 15.7-17.3 — the countdown mini-app full screen in the phone, numbers ticking.
7. 17.6-21 — "Add your friends": real photo avatars pop around "friends", drift, fly off;
   one avatar stays, shrinks, and lands in the chat as the member who joined.
8. 22-24 — group chat, hearts float up from a message.
9. 27.8-30.5 — "Life admin?": the word splits and emoji (grimace, card, calendar) squeeze
   between the letters, then close up; "Handled." appends.
10. 30.6-35 — agent writes first: "Your Comcast bill went up $41 a month. Want me to try to
    renegotiate it?" User: black pill "yes please". "On it." "Done. Back to $79/month —
    saving you $492 a year." Then the Bills mini-app, total ticking down.
11. 36.5-40 — "the right outcome / in the right interface", word by word.
12. 40-44 — a giant vertical word wheel behind the phone (mini-apps, trackers, games,
    experiments) scrolls one step per app while the phone shows each app.
13. 44-55 — "what about donkeys?" -> the agent makes a shared pet mini-app, hearts.
14. 55.6-61 — "Some apps last a day" -> everything after "Some" blurs away -> "Some grow
    with you".
15. 62-67 — home screen: mini-app cards lift out of the phone in 3D and land as widgets;
    an arc of app icons flows around the phone ("home screen that's truly yours").
16. 70-72 — "iOS. Android."
17. 72-75.5 — back to the liquid world: spheres roll along a groove, turn black, gather into
    the 5-dot logo, which becomes the app icon, which shrinks into "The all-new [icon] wabi
    app", then "[icon] wabi.ai".

## What to steal
- The agent writes first, the human answers two words ("yes please"), the agent reports the
  result with a number that matters to a person ($492 a year). That is the emotional core.
- Content first, container after: the conversation reads as a moment of life on white; the
  interface only forms around it afterwards.
- A question answered by one word: "Life admin? Handled." with objects squeezed into the word.
- Keep the first word, swap the rest ("Some apps last a day" -> "Some grow with you").
- Brand world bookends, and the logo is born from that world at the end.
