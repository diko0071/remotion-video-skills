## Language (what makes it expensive)

- ONE window on a soft sky (blurred clouds on blue, drifting slowly). Every
  scene change is either a camera move on that window, a dissolve (opacity +
  blur, never a hard cut) or a whiteout into a text beat. Zero cuts.
- Camera: spring-eased push-ins on the region that matters (composer 1 → 2.2x,
  huddle button 1 → 3x), one zoom-out to the whole window, then push-ins on
  the call panel and the transcript. Never a pull-back inside a beat; every
  pull-back is the start of a new beat.
- Objects: the window fades+scales in (0.96 → 1); the call window FLIES in
  from the bottom-left with a slight rotation (-6° → 0) and scale (0.6 → 1),
  motion-blurred; the controls bar slides up under it; the transcript panel
  drops from under the call window and its lines stream in one by one.
- Text beats on pure white: words settle one after another (SettleLine),
  avatars/agents pop ABOVE the line as a small cluster, the previous line
  fades as the next one starts. Endcard: sentence typed word by word, then
  the logo fades in.
- Cursor is a real macOS arrow, travels on a curve, hovers before the click,
  the button darkens on press.
