# blg-kinetic — BabyLoveGrowth kinetic typography ad (FB Ads Library 997591782721745)

Source: `source.mp4` (720x1280 vertical, 9.5s, ~126 BPM beat, no voiceover).
Frames: `frames/contact-4fps.png` (whole film), `frames/transition-30fps.png`
(word swap close-up).

## Arc
- **Hook (0-2.3s):** full-bleed BRAND ORANGE. "Why choose / BabyLoveGrowth?"
  typewrites character by character, white bold round grotesque, centered,
  two lines. No cursor visible — pure typewriter.
- **Kinetic words (2.3-8.0s):** screen split into two vertical stripes —
  BLACK always left, WHITE always right, split near 50/50. Twelve words,
  one per beat (~0.47s each), hard swap ON the beat. Words alternate
  stripes: word 1 white-on-black, word 2 black-on-white. The stripe border
  springs a little wider toward the active side on every swap — the
  "shimmering blinds" feel. Each word sits centered in ITS stripe,
  vertically centered on screen, ~44px bold at 720w.
- **Close (8.0-9.5s):** full white. "Try / [logo]BabyLoveGrowth.ai" centered,
  then "Today." types on a third line one beat later. Tail dips to black.

## The 12 words = 6 two-word phrases, one word per frame-beat
Keyword / Analysis · Daily / Content · Free / Backlinks · AI / Visibility ·
Reddit / Opportunities · All On / Autopilot.

First word of each pair lands in the black stripe, second in the white —
the pair reads as a phrase across two beats. Closer keeps the period:
"Autopilot."

## Steal
- Beat-locked one-word-per-stripe machine gun; phrases split across beats.
- Stripe border that breathes toward the active word (spring, not static).
- Typewriter hook on solid brand color; typewriter reprise on the endcard
  ("Today." types in) — the film opens and closes with the same mechanic.
- Total runtime discipline: 9.5s, three movements, zero parked frames.
