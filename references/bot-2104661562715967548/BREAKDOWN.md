## Grammar
- Only UI objects on flat grounds, never an app window. One anchor object per world
  (a bubble), everything else assembles around it and leaves before the next world.
- Colour = identity. Each role owns a ground colour, a dark bubble colour and an avatar
  colour, and the same colours come back in the payoff notifications.
- Copy is short and concrete: tasks in the teammate's own words, answers with a name,
  a number or a ticket id.
- Calm pace (2-3s per world), constant slow camera drift, staggered pops with a little
  overshoot. Grainy colour squares are the one texture motif.
- The bot is a character: it blinks, looks, grows from a dot to a hero avatar, ends as
  the logo.

## What we took for "Your dot's night shift" (and what we did not)
Took: one anchor message per world with a collage of real artefacts around it, a colour
per chapter, short copy with numbers, a character with eyes, a result card with buttons.
Did not take: the create-a-bot opening, role worlds, the working state, the kinetic
"Bring into Slack" line, Slack notifications, the pastel palette, grainy squares, the
bloub. Our story runs on a clock through one night and ends with the Ryze sunrise.
