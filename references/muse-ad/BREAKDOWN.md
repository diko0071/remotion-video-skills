## What it is
Live-action lifestyle. NO mascot, NO character for the agent. The agent is a
tiny chip in the corner of a human life: one line "Doing X" with a small
card, then a blue check "X done". The HUMAN is the hero; the product never
takes over the frame. Every task chip = the same three-state pattern:
avatar-dot + present-continuous line -> card -> check + past-tense line.
Cadence: one task per ~3s, four tasks, then the payoff is not a screen but
an evening with friends.

## What to steal
- The chip grammar: "Doing X" -> tiny card -> check "X done", present tense
  to past tense. Cheap, legible over any footage.
- The payoff is a human life, not a dashboard.
- The pull-back through the window as the endcard.

## The launch film (web/launch.mp4, 82s, 1920x1080, x.com/Muse/status/2097399178376671666)
Sheets at 1fps: web/l01-l05.png. Crops: web/treadmill.png, web/c-idle.png.

THE CHARACTER. Muse has a mascot: a plush beige yeti-like blob ("Polly" by
default, user-renamable, regenerable), fuzzy felt/velour 3D render, round
head with a hood-shaped body, two dot eyes, pink cheeks, tiny smile, stubby
arms. Three uses:
1. Inline in headlines as a word-sized sprite: "Muse [sprite] is your personal
   AI agent" (it blinks and squishes in place between words).
2. The chat header: a round avatar with a pill under it, "Muse" / "Muse is
   working" / "Muse · Creating shopping list" / "Waiting for permission" —
   the pill's status line is the only state indicator. When working it wears
   HEADPHONES (a laptop pose in the avatar). Typing dots below.
3. The payoff scene (~47-50s): full-body, running on a treadmill under a
   confetti rain, eyes closed then a determined face, after "You're in.
   Let's gooo!!". The ONLY full-body shot in the film. ~3s.
Also: the sprite rides inside a calendar tile ("Updated flight SFO to DEN"),
and stands in the "Your [sprite] Muse gets it done" headline with confetti.

Film structure: icon → object field → "Introducing Muse" → "Muse is your
personal AI agent" with emoji inlines → chat demos (school emails → shopping
list → order approval Deny/Allow → "Done!") → "AI that [emoji] goes shopping
/ drafts replies / is always working for you" refrains → training program →
10K signup via browser (Take control of the browser / Stop the task) →
treadmill payoff → sleep, plans, flight delay rebooked → calendar → "Your
Muse gets it done" → "AI that books trips / sends the gift / saves money" →
Connect your apps → checks "All in one place" → object field "It's your
Muse" → keeps you up-to-date / in the loop / in control → icon on black.
Ground: white with a soft blue gradient band at the bottom. Font: Optimistic.

What to steal: the character is a STATUS INDICATOR (pill under the avatar is
the whole UI of "working"); the full-body run is reserved for the one
emotional payoff; word-sized sprite inline in headlines.
