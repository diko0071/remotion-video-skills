## The text entrance (0.5s-3s and 10s-13s)

Serif line on a dark ground with slow light streaks. The sentence is laid out
in its final two-line position from the start; words rise into place one by
one from just below the baseline, out of a soft blur, at ~5-6 frame
intervals, with the last few words a touch faster. Inline product icon pops
into an already-open gap after the words around it have settled. No plates,
no sparks, no slide across the screen. The line holds, then fades softly
before the next object arrives.

This is exactly `kit/settle-text` (SettleLine), born from the earlier
Perplexity Portable Computer reference. Dmitry, 2026-09-04: this entrance is
now THE default for every text beat, endcards included.
