## What to steal
- A claim with a number, told as a counter (0% → 50%), then a concrete failure on screen
  (captcha blocks the agent) before the fix. Problem shown, not said.
- Named cursors: the agent is a cursor with a label, so every click has an actor.
- Status chips that resolve (Checking.. → Not found / Blocked / Agent-ready) are the
  whole proof layer; no dashboards.
- A line from the last word of a question leads the eye into the next beat.
