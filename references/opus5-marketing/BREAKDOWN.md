## Arc

1. 0-3s, dark: a search bar zooms up into "We kill… We killed [ChatGPT][Perplexity][Claude app icons]" typed inside the bar, icons pop in as tiles.
2. 3-6s: icons shrink into a row over "They promised us to do the work", the icons drop into a trash can.
3. 6-9s: "Instead, they bury you in" + a mini dock (Safari, Mail, Gemini, trash), cursor clicks → a stack of "Connect App +" buttons avalanches with motion blur → "buttons…".
4. 9-15s: "Then, they stop when it gets real" → a dark composer, user asks, the answer list fills with refusals ("I can't do that", "Please approve action", "I can't sign in to websites", "I can't do sensitive tasks"), "Why did you stop?", the refusals peel off and get dragged into the trash.

10. 66-80s, white: privacy. "everything is 🔒 private under your control", "Data never leaves your device", a MacBook closes, "runs locally, encrypt…" (scramble text), "Aside won't let AI read_your_passwords" in a field, a cursor toggles the eye and it masks to ******.

## Mechanics worth taking

- Problem told as a small comedy of the incumbents: named apps die in a trash can, "Connect App" buttons pile up, refusals get thrown away. Our version: marketing tools and "AI" that only drafts.
- Typography IS the product state: a tool call is a big centered line that shrinks into a status row as the next begins; result lists expand under a heading (Credit card ⌄).
- One long real task with a real outcome and a number ($120 refund). The payoff is a user reaction line, not a claim.
- Benchmark bars as the proof beat, our model highlighted, competitors named.
- Word slot machine for breadth.
- Colour rhythm: dark (problem) → light-curtain reveal → white (product) → dark (proof) → white (trust) → dark (close).
- Every icon is a real app mark, cursor clicks everything.
